NILE FREIGHT LINES - DRIVER HANDBOOK (EXCERPT, 2026 EDITION)

1. SAFETY EQUIPMENT
Drivers must wear a high-visibility safety vest and steel-toe boots at all times when outside the cab at shippers, receivers, and port terminals. Hard hats are required at the Savannah Port Terminal. A fire extinguisher and three reflective warning triangles must be in the cab and checked during every pre-trip inspection.

2. HOURS OF SERVICE
Drivers follow federal hours-of-service rules using the electronic logging device (ELD) installed in every truck. The maximum driving time is 11 hours after 10 consecutive hours off duty, and drivers may not drive beyond the 14th hour after coming on duty. A 30-minute break is required after 8 cumulative hours of driving. Dispatch will never ask a driver to exceed these limits; report any such request to the safety manager.

3. DETENTION REPORTING
Detention is billable when a driver waits more than 2 hours at a pickup or delivery. Drivers must record arrival and departure times in the ELD and on the bill of lading, and send a photo of the signed BOL to dispatch within 1 hour of leaving the facility. Detention that is not documented this way cannot be billed to the customer.

4. ACCIDENTS AND INCIDENTS
In any accident, first make sure everyone is safe and call 911 if anyone is injured. Then call the 24-hour safety line at 912-555-0199. Do not admit fault or discuss the accident with anyone except police and company safety staff. Take photos of all vehicles, the scene, and any damage before vehicles are moved, if it is safe to do so.

5. FUEL CARDS
Company fuel cards may only be used for diesel and DEF for the assigned truck. Personal purchases are prohibited. Lost or stolen cards must be reported to dispatch immediately so the card can be blocked.
